The leaderboard inverts. k3-greedy wins on published score and loses on answer quality. It converges more often — and is wrong more often when it does. The config we promoted didn't buy accuracy; it converted hangs into confident wrong answers.

FINDINGS

  • TR3's maths failures are termination failures. Across all four configs there is exactly one wrong maths answer. Every other maths miss is the model producing 32,768 tokens without concluding. Three of the four score 100% on maths items that terminate.
  • Spec-decode config does not change capability. deepctx lands at 7, 7, 7, 8 across every config. What moves is whether a failure surfaces as a wrong answer or as a hang — a scheduling property, not a reasoning one.
  • Prefill is invariant. 0.4% spread across configs that differ by 36% on decode. Any future "faster prefill" claim from a spec-decode change is a measurement artifact.
  • TR3 regressed on long context. The EXL3 it replaced scores 10/15 deepctx with zero non-convergence; every TR3 config lands at 7–8. The quantisation bought throughput and cost retrieval — invisible until non-convergence was split out.
  • Recommendation: mtp5-prob. Fastest at 97 tok/s, second on answer quality, mid on both failure modes. k3-greedy's extra point is bought in the wrong currency for an agentic estate, where a confident wrong answer propagates into the next tool call and a hang does not.
