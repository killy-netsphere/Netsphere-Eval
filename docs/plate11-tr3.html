Prefill favours TR3 too — 129s to first token at 263K against the hybrid's 140s at 254K, roughly 2,050 vs 1,810 tok/s.

On accuracy these three are a wash — 90.8%, 91.0%, 92.1% once non-convergence is separated out. The published totals differ by 2 points and the differences are almost entirely termination behaviour, not reasoning. Which means the decision is not an accuracy decision: it is throughput, pool and reach.

A wider bank sharpens the point. Across three 99-item arms, wrong answers held at 3, 3 and 2 while non-convergence ran 0, 3 and 11 — converged-only accuracy landed within 0.8 points of itself everywhere. The serving config does not change this model's accuracy. It changes how often it terminates.

FINDINGS

  • TR3's maths failures are termination failures. Across every TR3 config tested there is exactly one wrong maths answer. Every other maths miss is 32,768 tokens without a conclusion.
  • The hybrid's weakness is deep context. 6/15 on deepctx against TR3's 7/15, and it is the only one of the three to drop a grounded item. Its 20/20 on maths is the one place it clearly leads.
  • The pool is the other half of the win. TR3 loads 883,685 KV tokens against the hybrid's 359,424 — 2.46×, read from each engine's own kv_cache_utils line at startup. That is what buys the 392K window the hybrid cannot open, and it is why the decode advantage still holds at depths the hybrid never sees.
  • TR3 is also the smaller quantisation. EXL3 Trellis at 3.0 bpw on routed experts with BF16 attention and dense layers, against the hybrid's 3.25 bpw average — 75% in-house NF3 3-bit, 25% NVFP4, on a custom kernel no upstream stack ships. Smaller weights, standard kernels, larger pool, more speed.
  • Recommendation: mtp5-prob. Fastest at every depth, best accuracy-when-it-answers, and the only config that reaches 392K at full speed. The stock default is a perfectly respectable fallback — it already matches the hybrid — but leaves 20%+ of decode on the table.

All figures measured on one host: 4× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell at TP4/DCP4, gpu-memory-utilization 0.96, fp8 KV, memory offset +6000.
