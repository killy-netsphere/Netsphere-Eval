It Didn't Get Smarter. It Got Obedient.

DeepSeek shipped a new V4-Flash checkpoint. Our suite said +10 points. Re-scoring the same stored answers said +2. The gap between those two numbers is the whole story — and it is a story about the harness, not the model.

PREFILL — AND WHERE IT PEAKS

PREFILL THROUGHPUT vs CONTEXTtok/s · TTFT-derived

Prefill peaks at 32K, not at 4K. Short prompts cannot fill the batch — the GPUs are idle waiting for work. By 128K the batch is saturated and throughput starts paying for attention over a longer sequence, falling to 3,506 tok/s by 761K. A full 761K prefill takes just over two minutes before the first token appears.

Overall 74/80 vs 64/80, +12.5pp, exact McNemar p=0.0129. Read at face value: the new checkpoint is markedly better at grounding and at hard maths. That reading is wrong.

HOW IT FAILED

SCORER · no_final_answer_line

Nine correct answers, scored as wrong.

Every scored task ends with the same instruction: end your reply with a line reading FINAL ANSWER: <answer>. The scorer looks for that line and, if it is absent, fails the item without ever comparing the content:

ans = extract_final(r["content"])          # last /FINAL ANSWER:\s*(.+)/ match
if ans is None:
    return False, "no_final_answer_line"   # never looks at the answer

The 0711 arm answered many items correctly and tersely — 18, 3952, NOT IN CONTEXT — with no trailing line. Nine such items were marked wrong on formatting alone. The 0731 arm did it once.

This is not a bug that invents a winner — the difference is real. The instruction is explicit and followable, and one model followed it nine times more reliably than the other. What the score gets wrong is which skill it credits: seven of those nine sit in a category called grounded_v2, and not one of them was a grounding error. Format compliance was silently filed as reasoning ability.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE

  • Reasoning is a tie. On content, 75 vs 73 out of 80 — p=0.6875, null. The two genuine gains are single items: one where the old arm ran out of tokens mid-calculation, one where it misread an O for a Q.
  • Instruction compliance is not a tie — it is 9 to 1. That is the largest real difference between these checkpoints, and for a model driving tool calls, structured proposals and worker JSON, it may matter more than the reasoning parity does.
  • Deep context did not move: 10/15 on both arms, with an identical needle-depth profile (3/5 · 4/5 · 3/5). Only 2 of 100 responses hit the token ceiling, so this is a capability ceiling and not a budget artifact. It is the one number in this plate that no scoring choice changes.
  • The board needs re-scoring. Every model on the v2 leaderboard was ranked by this rule, so all of them are ranked partly on how reliably they emit a literal string. The transcripts are on disk; the correction costs no inference.
